Consent to Personal Data Processing

Effective date: March 27, 2026 · Internet Com LLC, TIN 01810202310575

1. Scope of Consent

By submitting data through the job application form on the Careers page of the inter-com.online website or by sending an email to info@inter-com.online, you provide Internet Com LLC (hereinafter — "Company") with consent to process your personal data in accordance with this document and the Privacy Policy.

2. Data Processed

The following personal data may be processed:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Desired job position
  • Cover letter and any other information voluntarily provided
  • File name of an attached resume

3. Purposes of Processing

Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • Reviewing job applications and communicating with candidates
  • Responding to inquiries sent by email
  • Maintaining records of applicants for currently open and future positions

4. Data Retention

Personal data submitted via email is retained for as long as necessary for the purposes specified above, or for the period required by applicable law, but no longer than 3 years from the date of submission, unless the data subject withdraws consent earlier.

5. Withdrawal of Consent

You have the right to withdraw consent to personal data processing at any time by sending a written request to: info@inter-com.online. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to the withdrawal.

6. Contact

For questions related to personal data processing, contact: info@inter-com.online
