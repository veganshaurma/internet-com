About Us

An IT team specializing in software development, quality assurance, solution architecture and technical documentation for digital products.

Who we are

Internet Com LLC

Internet Com LLC (ООО «Интернет Ком») is an IT team engaged in custom software development, functional and regression testing, solution architecture design, technical documentation preparation, and digital project support.

We work on projects of varying scale: corporate internal systems, web and backend solutions, multi-component platforms. Our approach is built on careful planning, engineering discipline, and transparent communication with clients.

The company is registered in the Kyrgyz Republic and works with both local and international clients, communicating in Russian and English.

Approach

How We Work

Our approach is built on engineering principles that ensure predictability and quality of results.

Careful Planning
Every project starts with a detailed requirements analysis. We don't begin implementation without a clear understanding of the task and an agreed plan.
Engineering Discipline
Code reviews, development standards, version control — these are not extras, but mandatory parts of our process.
Transparent Communication
Regular progress reports, clear task definitions and timely notification of risks or changes.
Requirements Focus
We document and verify every requirement, track changes and align them with the client before implementation.
Quality Control
QA specialists are involved from the beginning of the project. Testing is a continuous process, not a final stage.
Documentation First
Everything important is recorded: architectural decisions, API contracts, test cases. The client receives a complete documentation package.
